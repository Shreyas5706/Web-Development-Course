AHMEDABAD METRO
Ahmedabad Metro is a rapid transit system for the cities of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar in Gujarat state of India.
Metro Timings: Phase 1 of the Ahmedabad Metro runs from 6:20 AM to 10 PM.
Route : APMC to Motera Stadium via Shreyas, Old high Court , Vadaj , Ranip and Sabarmati.
Upcoming Stations :As of July 2024, the following stations on the Ahmedabad Metro are under construction: Kankaria East, Thaltej Gam, and Sabarmati.
